\section{Data Construction Details}\label{app:data}

\subsection{PBoC depositor survey processing}

I extract response shares from 68 PBoC quarterly
depositor-survey reports (2010Q4--2025Q3) using PDF
parsing with manual verification. Each report records
the fraction of respondents selecting ``prices will
rise,'' ``stay the same,'' ``fall,'' and ``hard to
say.'' Raw shares are stored in
\texttt{data/intermediate/pboc\_depositor\_survey\_quarterly.csv}.

The Carlson--Parkin quantification
(Appendix~\ref{app:measurement}) is implemented in
\texttt{src/05\_carlson\_parkin\_quantify.R}, which
outputs three expectation series (benchmark, lower
bound, upper bound) and a cross-validated $\delta$
parameter to
\texttt{data/intermediate/pboc\_expected\_inflation\_cp.csv}.

\subsection{Macroeconomic data}

NBS data are downloaded via the EasyQuery API
(\texttt{src/04\_download\_nbs\_easyquery.py}).
Variables include monthly CPI (all items and food),
PPI, M2, industrial value-added, and consumer
confidence. Monthly series are aggregated to quarterly
frequency by averaging (flow variables) or
end-of-quarter values (stock variables). The processed
panel is stored in
\texttt{data/processed/panel\_quarterly.csv}.

The China EPU index is sourced from
\citet{Baker2016} (\texttt{src/02\_download\_epu.py})
and the GPR index from \citet{Caldara2022}
(\texttt{src/01\_download\_gpr.py}). Both are monthly
and averaged to quarters.

\subsection{CFPS data construction}

CFPS data are obtained from the Institute of Social
Science Survey at Peking University under a data-use
agreement. Seven waves (2010, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018,
2020, 2022) are stored as Stata \texttt{.dta} files in
\texttt{data/raw/CFPS/}. The processing script
(\texttt{src/12\_build\_cfps\_panel.R}) proceeds as
follows:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Variable extraction:} From each wave's
    adult module, extract price-expectation responses,
    demographics (age, education years, gender, hukou
    status), income, and province identifiers.
  \item \textbf{Panel linkage:} Use the CFPS cross-year
    identifier (\texttt{ecfps2022crossyear\_202601.dta})
    to link individuals across waves, enabling revision
    construction.
  \item \textbf{Expectation coding:} Code price
    expectations as $+1$ (rise), $0$ (same), $-1$ (fall).
    ``Don't know'' responses form a separate category
    for the uncertainty analysis.
  \item \textbf{Provincial CPI merge:} Match each
    household to its province's CPI inflation between
    waves, constructing a household-level forecast-error
    proxy.
  \item \textbf{Sample restrictions:} Restrict to adults
    aged 18--70 with non-missing price expectations in
    at least two consecutive waves.
\end{enumerate}

The output is a household-wave panel
(\texttt{data/processed/cfps\_panel.csv}) with
approximately 80,000 observations.

\subsection{Carlson--Parkin threshold sensitivity}

Table~\ref{tab:delta_sensitivity} reports the diagnostic
coefficient under alternative perception thresholds
$\delta \in \{0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0\}$. The baseline
uses $\delta = 0.5$, equal to the sample-average
quarterly annualized CPI inflation rate. The sign of
$\beta_1$ is negative across all thresholds, with
magnitudes ranging from $-0.44$ ($\delta = 0.3$) to
$-0.57$ ($\delta = 1.0$). This insensitivity arises
because $\delta$ scales both revision and forecast
error, leaving their ratio approximately invariant.
